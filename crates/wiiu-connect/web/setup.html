Wii U Connect – Setup

Wii U Connect sets up the Cemu emulator and your phones. It never downloads games, keys or Nintendo system files. Use dumps of games you own, for example made with Dumpling on your own Wii U.

Setting everything up

    1

    Cemu emulator

    Checking…
    Official release from github.com/cemu-project/Cemu (free, open source).
    I already have Cemu
    2

    Controllers

    Cemu's Controller 1 becomes the phone GamePad and Controllers 2–4 the phone Wii Remotes (players 1–3). Your own settings are backed up first. This happens automatically on start.
    3

    Your games

    Cemu opens .wua files and unpacked games (code/, content/, meta/) directly. Encrypted disc images (.wud/.wux) also need your console's keys in Cemu's keys.txt.
    4

    Phones

    Scan this with each phone's camera (same Wi-Fi as this PC), then choose GamePad or Wii Remote. The phone warns about the certificate once: continue anyway.
